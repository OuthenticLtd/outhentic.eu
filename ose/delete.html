Request data deletion - OSE (Outhentic Sound Engine)

OSE collects only one piece of data off your device: a one-way hashed device identifier, stored solely to prevent the same device from repeatedly claiming the free trial. No account, name, email, or personal data is collected.

How to request deletion

Email ose@outhentic.eu from any address with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete your device's trial record. (Because the value we store is an anonymous one-way hash not linked to any identity, simply emailing us is enough - we clear the record on request.)

Email ose@outhentic.eu to delete my data

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • What is deleted: the hashed device-trial record.
  • What is kept: nothing else is stored server-side. All your recordings, instruments, and settings live only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Retention: the hashed record is kept only as long as needed to enforce the one-trial-per-device limit; deleted on request.

More detail on what we process and your GDPR rights is in our Privacy policy. Questions: ose@outhentic.eu.
