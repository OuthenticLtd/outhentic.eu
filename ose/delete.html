Request data deletion - OSE (Outhentic Sound Engine)

OSE is an on-device music app by Outhentic Ltd. The only data held off your device is a one-way hashed device identifier, stored solely to prevent the same device from repeatedly claiming the free 3-day Pro trial. No account, name, email, or other personal data is collected. There are two kinds of data you can delete, and a different step for each.

1. On-device content (your recordings, instruments, MIDI, presets, settings)

All of your content lives only on your device. To remove it, uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android settings (Settings → Apps → OSE → Storage → Clear data). This deletes everything the app stored on your device. We never receive this content, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

2. The trial-prevention hash held by us

To delete the one-way hashed device identifier we hold, email ose@outhentic.eu with the subject "Delete my data". We will remove the Firebase record within 30 days, free of charge. Because the value we store is an anonymous one-way hash not linked to any name, email, or account, emailing us is enough for us to clear it on request.

Email ose@outhentic.eu to delete my data

Please note: deleting the hash makes that device eligible for the free Pro trial again.

More detail on what we process and your GDPR rights is in our Privacy policy. Questions: ose@outhentic.eu.
