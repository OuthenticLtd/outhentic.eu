Troubleshooting checklist

When something isn't working, walk this list top-to-bottom. It catches the majority of issues in under a minute.

No signal on Tuner / Meters / Audio

  1. Open Settings → Audio. Confirm the input device matches what you've got plugged in.
  2. If using a USB interface, the channel chooser sits inside the same sheet — pick the channel your microphone or instrument is connected to.
  3. Open the Meters module. If the bars move you've got signal — the issue is downstream.

Crackles or feedback while recording

  1. Turn MONITOR off — feedback into the same chain you're printing causes runaway loops.
  2. Lower the system output level; some devices distort their own loopback past −6 dB.
  3. If using USB, check the cable. Bus-powered interfaces sometimes brown out under heavy DSP.

USB device disappears mid-session

  1. OSE recovers automatically through Android's audio device callbacks; give it a couple of seconds.
  2. If it still doesn't reappear, unplug and replug the device. The picker re-populates on hotplug.

Edits aren't saving

  1. Audio edits are virtual until you Bounce. Tap the Bounce button in the Audio Editor to commit.
  2. If a take seems lost, the original is still in the take's stash — Revert restores it.
