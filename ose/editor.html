Newsletter signup

The signup form in the footer collects emails for your newsletter. Two delivery modes:

  • Mailto (default) - leave Endpoint URL empty. When a visitor submits, their email client opens pre-filled to your contact email. They click send. Each signup arrives in your inbox; search your inbox later and copy/paste into a CSV for Mailchimp import.
  • Endpoint URL - paste a Formspree, Cloudflare Worker, or self-hosted URL. Submissions POST as JSON like {"email": "...", "name": "...", "page": "...", "ts": "..."}. Mailto fallback fires automatically if the endpoint errors.

Spam protection is local: a hidden honeypot field, a 3-second minimum fill time, and basic email validation. No captcha.
